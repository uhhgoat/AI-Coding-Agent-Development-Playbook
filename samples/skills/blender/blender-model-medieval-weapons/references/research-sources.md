# Medieval Weapon Research Ledger

Research pass: `2026-07-28`.

Use these sources for workflow comparison, not as permission to copy a weapon
design or redistribute project files.

## Instructional sources with files or staged workflows

- [CG Fast Track: Blender Fast Track downloads](https://www.cgfasttrack.com/blender-fast-track2-old)
  links a free multi-part sword tutorial, separate project/download files, a
  sword download, and a hotkey sheet. Transferable lessons include explicit
  project setup, reference-driven blockout, separate tutorial stages, and
  recoverable downloads.
- [Blender 2.8 Medieval Shield collaboration](https://www.blendernation.com/2019/10/22/blender-2-8-shield-modeling-collaboration-grant-abbit-jayanam/)
  separates modeling, UV unwrapping, and texture painting across three parts.
  Transferable lesson: do not collapse geometry, UV, and surface approval into
  one vague “finished” pass.
- [Brainchild: Low Poly Shield in Blender](https://brainchild.artstation.com/blog/YpEr/low-poly-shield-in-blender-2-90-3d-weapon-design-part-1-blender-3d-basics-beginner)
  is a stylized RPG shield modeling tutorial and links a broader weapon
  playlist. Use it as low-poly silhouette and game-readability evidence.
- [Blender Studio: Low Poly Character Creation](https://studio.blender.org/training/low-poly-character-creation/chapter/5978699a8119172dda22c7cb/)
  includes both high- and low-poly prop modeling, UV work, baking, and project
  stages. Although the demonstrated prop is modern, the high/low separation
  and bake workflow transfer to weapons.
- [Blender Manual: Array Modifier](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/generate/array.html)
  is the authority for repeated geometry, offsets, merge behavior, caps, and
  local-scale caveats used by flanged or wrapped weapon parts.
- [Blender Manual: Mirror Modifier](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/generate/mirror.html)
  is the authority for object-space symmetry, clipping, merging, and mirror
  centers.
- [Blender Manual: Bevel Modifier](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/generate/bevel.html)
  is the authority for controlled non-destructive edge treatment.

## Findings adopted by this skill

- Project files are most useful when their tutorial stages can be compared,
  not when the final mesh is merely available.
- Reference images constrain profiles; cross-sections and attachment still
  need independent construction decisions.
- Modeling, UVs, baking, and texture painting should remain separately
  reviewable.
- Symmetry belongs in object space and must not be inferred from perspective.
- Repeated flanges, studs, and wrap elements benefit from retained parametric
  controls.
- Low/high pairs can preserve a game mesh while moving fine detail into baked
  maps.

## Rights and quality rules

- Use original creator/course pages. Do not use unauthorized mirrors or
  repackaged paid tutorials.
- Record source URL, author, license, retrieval date, and downloadable-file
  checksum.
- Open downloaded `.blend` files with auto-execution disabled and classify
  their evidence before inspection.
- Do not use assets marked `NoAI` for automated reconstruction or skill
  derivation.
- Do not claim historical accuracy from stylized tutorial output. Add museum,
  conservation, measured-replica, or other authoritative construction sources
  when historical accuracy becomes an acceptance criterion.
